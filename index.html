Section 3.4 — End-to-End Patient Journey

Five-Step Patient Consultation Journey

From kiosk arrival to physician examination: minimal hospital staff assistance.

1

Step 1 — Identify

Patient enters ABHA or skips identity as guest. Meets Dr. Meera AI assistant.

Time: ~45s
2

Step 2 — Converse

AI conducts adaptive voice + touch history interview (SOCRATES or AYUSH). Red flags trigger triage.

Time: ~2m
3

Step 3 — Scan

Patient scans prescriptions & lab reports. OCR extracts entities, flags abnormal values.

Time: ~1m
4

Step 4 — Route

AI synthesizes summary, links to ABHA PHR, pushes FHIR to HIS, prints OPD token receipt.

Time: ~10s
5

Step 5 — Consult

Physician reads complete history in 15 seconds, devotes consultation to physical exam.

Saved: 65% Time
