Section 3.4 — End-to-End Patient Journey

Five-Step Patient Consultation Flow

From arrival to physician consultation with minimal hospital staff assistance.

1

Step 1 — Identify

Patient logs in via ABHA ID or Aadhaar, selects preferred language, and grants audio-guided consent.

Time: ~45s
2

Step 2 — Converse

AI conducts adaptive voice + touch history interview. Emergency red-flag symptoms trigger immediate triage.

Time: ~2 mins
3

Step 3 — Scan

Patient scans prescriptions and lab reports. AI extracts entities, highlights abnormal values, and builds timeline.

Time: ~1 min
4

Step 4 — Route

AI synthesizes clinical summary, links to ABHA PHR, pushes FHIR bundle to HIS, and prints token slip.

Time: ~10s
5

Step 5 — Consult

Physician reads complete history in 15 seconds, verifies draft, and devotes full consultation to clinical examination.

Saved: 65% OPD Time
